# Moroccan restaurant menu templates

## Goal

Provide complete, distinct menu designs inspired by Moroccan restaurant styles, rather than cosmetic variations of the existing Classic, Modern, and Minimal layouts.

## Scope

- Add three Moroccan restaurant presets: Medina Café, Moroccan Fine Dining, and Modern Moroccan Garden.
- Give each preset its own page composition, typography, color system, section and subsection presentation, and image treatment.
- Include sample menu data with categories, dishes, descriptions, prices, and food imagery.
- Support Arabic right-to-left rendering.
- Show unavailable dishes as sold out across every template.
- Preserve the existing template IDs and behavior where compatible.

## Acceptance criteria

- All six templates can be selected and previewed in the builder.
- The three Moroccan templates have visibly different full-menu layouts and include sections, subsections, and food imagery.
- Arabic menu content renders right-to-left.
- Unavailable items are clearly marked sold out in all templates.
- Existing template IDs remain valid.
