# Add full Moroccan restaurant menu templates

Status: in progress

## Goal

Ship three distinct Moroccan restaurant menu presets with full sections, subsections, food imagery, Arabic RTL support, and sold-out item states while preserving the three existing template IDs.

## Acceptance criteria

- The builder offers Medina Café, Moroccan Fine Dining, and Modern Moroccan Garden as distinct complete templates.
- Each includes a complete sample menu structure with sections, subsections, dish details, and imagery.
- Arabic menu content renders right-to-left.
- Unavailable menu items show as sold out across all templates.
- Existing template IDs remain valid.
