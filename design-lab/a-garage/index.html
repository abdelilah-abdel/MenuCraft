The problem, on an empty wall

A printed menu costs $500–$2,000 every redesign. Menu services cost $50–$200 a month, on a contract.

MenuCraft is free to start and takes five minutes. That is the whole pitch — the rest of this page is the machine that keeps that promise.

Physical menu aesthetic

The editor feels like paper stock and a ruled job card, not like a website builder. Menus come out looking like menus.

Workbench simplicity

Sections, items, prices, colours. No design skills, no training, no manual to read before lunch service.

Generous free tier

One menu, unlimited items, QR codes and public links — free forever. Food trucks run on it as-is.

Hand-crafted templates

Six designs drawn for food: Classic, Modern, Minimal, Medina Café, Moroccan Fine Dining, Modern Moroccan Garden.
